# Data sources

The content in `modules.csv` and `knowledge_base.csv` was collected from public pages of
the University of Wolverhampton website (https://www.wlv.ac.uk) on 1 October 2026 and
rewritten in summary form. Check each item against the live page before relying on it:
dates, phone numbers and opening hours change.
